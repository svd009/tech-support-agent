# VPN connection problems

Source ID: VPN-001
Audience: All employees
Environment: Fictional training company

How to connect: open the company VPN app, choose the "Corporate" profile and sign in with your
corporate username and password. Approve the MFA prompt only if you started that sign-in.

What the support agent checks and fixes automatically: VPN service status, your network
adapter, IP lease and DNS, account lock, password expiry, VPN licence, device certificate,
client version, connection profile and stuck gateway sessions.

If your password has expired, the agent sends you a one-time reset link. A missing licence or
an active VPN outage is passed to a technician automatically.

Never share passwords or MFA codes. Do not disable security software.
