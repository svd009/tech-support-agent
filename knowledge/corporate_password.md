# Corporate password reset

Source ID: PWD-001
Audience: All employees
Environment: Fictional training company

Your corporate password is used for Windows sign-in, VPN and email. To reset it, tell the
support agent "I need to reset my password". The agent checks your account, unlocks it if it
is locked and gives you a personal one-time link that is valid for 15 minutes.

New passwords need at least 12 characters and three of: uppercase, lowercase, digits, symbols.
After the reset, reply "done" in chat so the agent can verify it, then sign in again on your
laptop, VPN and phone mail app. Never type a password into the chat.

Can't sign in to this Support Desk at all? Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page instead.
