# Email and Outlook

Source ID: MAIL-001
Audience: All employees
Environment: Fictional training company

The support agent checks email service status, account lock and password expiry, mailbox
licence, mailbox storage, the Outlook profile and mailbox sync. It can archive old mail when the
mailbox is full, rebuild a corrupt Outlook profile and reset a stuck sync.

After a fix, close and reopen Outlook and allow a minute for it to sync.
